Maths · dividing

Sharing
Fairly

Dividing is just sharing things out so that everybody gets the same.

You already know how to do this

Six cookies. Two plates. Put them out so it is fair.

6 ÷ 2 = 3

Six cookies shared between two plates is three on each plate.

Question one

Twelve cookies,
three friends

You know how many friends there are. You want to know how many each one gets.

12 ÷ 3 = 4

Twelve cookies shared between three friends is four cookies each.

Question two

The same twelve,
a different question

Now the bags hold four cookies each. You want to know how many bags you need.

12 ÷ 4 = 3

Twelve cookies, four to a bag, fills three bags.

Two questions,
one picture

Sharing out

12 ÷ 3 = 4

Making groups

12 ÷ 4 = 3

You knew each bag holds 4.
You found out you need 3 bags.

Same twelve cookies. Same three groups of four. But you were asking two different questions — so watch what the answer is counting.

Three rows of four cookies is twelve. So twelve shared into three rows must be four.
